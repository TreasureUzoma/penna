# How to Avoid Spam Folders

Getting your newsletters into the inbox instead of spam is crucial for engagement. This guide covers proven strategies to maximize deliverability.

## Why Emails Go to Spam

Spam filters evaluate multiple factors:

1. **Sender reputation** - Your domain and IP reputation
2. **Authentication** - SPF, DKIM, DMARC records
3. **Content quality** - Spam trigger words and patterns
4. **Engagement** - How recipients interact with your emails
5. **Infrastructure** - Proper email server configuration
6. **List quality** - Bounce rates and spam complaints

## Technical Setup (Critical)

### Step 1: Verify Your Domain

Custom domains have much better deliverability than generic ones:

✅ **Good:** newsletters@yourdomain.com  
❌ **Bad:** Using only Penna's shared sending domain

**Setup:**

1. Go to **Settings** → **Domain**
2. Add your custom domain
3. Add DNS records provided by Penna
4. Wait for verification (usually 24-48 hours)

### Step 2: Configure SPF Record

SPF (Sender Policy Framework) tells email servers which IPs can send from your domain.

**Get your SPF record from your Penna dashboard:**

1. Go to **Settings** → **Domain** → **DNS Records**
2. Copy the SPF value shown (includes Amazon SES)
3. Add as TXT record to your DNS

**Type:** TXT  
**Name:** @ (or your domain)  
**Value:** [Copy from dashboard]

**Note:** If you already have an SPF record, see your dashboard for merge instructions.

### Step 3: Set Up DKIM

DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail) digitally signs your emails to prove authenticity.

**Get your DKIM record from your Penna dashboard:**

1. Go to **Settings** → **Domain** → **DNS Records**
2. Copy the DKIM record (name and value)
3. Add as TXT record to your DNS
4. Wait for verification

### Step 4: Enable DMARC

DMARC tells email servers what to do if SPF/DKIM checks fail.

**Get your DMARC record from your Penna dashboard:**

1. Go to **Settings** → **Domain** → **DNS Records**
2. Copy the DMARC record value
3. Add as TXT record to your DNS

**Type:** TXT  
**Name:** \_dmarc.yourdomain.com  
**Value:** [Copy from dashboard]

### Step 5: Set Up rDNS (Reverse DNS)

Penna handles this automatically, but verify it's configured correctly.

## Content Best Practices

### Avoid Spam Trigger Words

These words and phrases commonly trigger spam filters:

❌ **Urgency/Pressure:**

- "Act now"
- "Limited time"
- "Urgent"
- "Don't wait"
- "Once in a lifetime"

❌ **Money/Free stuff:**

- "Make money fast"
- "Get paid"
- "Cash bonus"
- "Free money"
- "Prize"

❌ **Excessive claims:**

- "100% free"
- "Guaranteed"
- "No risk"
- "No obligation"
- "You won't believe"

❌ **Deceptive:**

- "Not spam"
- "This isn't junk"
- "Not a scam"

### Instead, Use:

✅ Descriptive, honest language  
✅ Specific value propositions  
✅ Professional tone  
✅ Clear, straightforward messaging

### Subject Line Guidelines

**Do:**

- Keep under 50 characters
- Be specific and relevant
- Use personalization sparingly
- Test different approaches

**Don't:**

- Use all caps
- Overuse exclamation marks (!!!)
- Use excessive emoji 🎉🎉🎉
- Mislead about content

**Examples:**

❌ "FREE MONEY NOW!!! Open immediately 💰💰💰"  
✅ "Your weekly development newsletter"

❌ "RE: Your account (urgent action required)"  
✅ "New features in our January update"

### Email Content Structure

**Good structure:**

```
Subject: Weekly Product Updates

Hi [Name],

Here's what's new this week:

1. New feature: Dark mode
2. Bug fixes in the editor
3. Performance improvements

[Read full changelog]

Thanks,
The Team

---
Unsubscribe
```

**Key elements:**

- Clear from name and address
- Personalization (optional, don't overdo)
- Valuable content upfront
- Clear call-to-action
- Unsubscribe link (required — *Note: Penna automatically appends a visible unsubscribe link and RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe` headers to all outgoing newsletters, so you don't need to manually include one in your content body.*)

### Formatting Tips

✅ **Use plain text or simple HTML** - Overly complex HTML triggers filters  
✅ **Balance text and images** - 80% text, 20% images is a good ratio  
✅ **Include alt text for images** - Helps accessibility and deliverability  
✅ **Avoid large attachments** - Link to files instead  
✅ **Test before sending** - Preview in multiple email clients

❌ **Don't use:**

- All images, no text
- Excessive colors
- Invisible text (white on white)
- Misleading formatting
- Forms in emails

## List Management

### Only Email Subscribers

**Never:**

- Buy email lists
- Scrape emails from websites
- Add people without permission
- Import contacts from other services without consent

**Always:**

- Use double opt-in (recommended)
- Get explicit consent
- Document how they subscribed
- Make unsubscribe easy

### Monitor Engagement

Spam filters track:

- Open rates
- Click rates
- Reply rates
- Delete rates
- Spam complaints

**Low engagement signals spam.** Keep your list engaged:

- Send valuable content
- Maintain consistent schedule
- Remove inactive subscribers
- Segment your audience

### Clean Your List Regularly

Remove or suppress:

- **Hard bounces** - Invalid email addresses
- **Repeated soft bounces** - Temporary issues that persist
- **Unsubscribes** - Respect opt-outs immediately
- **Spam complaints** - Never email again
- **Inactive users** - No opens/clicks in 6+ months (optional)

### Handle Bounces

Penna automatically manages bounces, but you should:

1. Monitor bounce rate (keep below 2%)
2. Investigate high bounce rates
3. Remove invalid emails quickly
4. Never retry hard bounces

## Sending Practices

### Warm Up Your Domain (New Senders)

If you're sending from a new domain:

**Week 1:** Send to 50-100 most engaged subscribers  
**Week 2:** Double the volume  
**Week 3:** Double again  
**Week 4:** Continue gradual increase

This builds sender reputation slowly.

### Maintain Consistent Volume

Sudden volume spikes trigger spam filters.

❌ **Don't:**

- Go from 100 to 10,000 emails overnight
- Skip months then send massive blast
- Send irregularly

✅ **Do:**

- Maintain steady sending schedule
- Gradually increase volume
- Send consistently

### Optimal Sending Frequency

There's no perfect frequency, but:

- **Too often:** Annoys subscribers, increases unsubscribes
- **Too rare:** Subscribers forget they signed up, mark as spam

**Recommended:**

- Weekly for most newsletters
- 2-3x week for news/updates
- Daily only for time-sensitive content

**Test and adjust based on:**

- Unsubscribe rate
- Engagement metrics
- Industry standards

### Best Time to Send

Varies by audience, but generally:

**Best days:** Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday  
**Best times:**

- 10 AM - 11 AM (workday start)
- 2 PM - 3 PM (post-lunch)
- 8 PM - 9 PM (evening)

**Test different times and track engagement.**

## Engagement Strategies

### Encourage Interaction

Higher engagement = better deliverability.

**Ask subscribers to:**

- Reply to your emails
- Click links in content
- Forward to friends
- Add you to contacts/address book
- Mark as "not spam" if in spam folder

### Write Compelling Subject Lines

Subject lines that get opens:

- Create curiosity without clickbait
- Promise clear value
- Use numbers and lists
- Keep it short
- A/B test different approaches

### Provide Value Consistently

Every email should give value:

- Educational content
- Exclusive insights
- Useful resources
- Early access
- Special offers (not every email)

### Make Content Scannable

Most people skim emails:

- Use short paragraphs
- Add subheadings
- Include bullet points
- Highlight key points
- Clear call-to-action

## Monitoring & Testing

### Track Key Metrics

Monitor these in your dashboard:

- **Delivery rate** - % successfully delivered (should be above 98%)
- **Bounce rate** - % bounced (should be below 2%)
- **Spam complaint rate** - % marked as spam (should be below 0.1%)
- **Open rate** - % opened (varies by industry, 15-25% is typical)
- **Click rate** - % clicked (2-5% is common)
- **Unsubscribe rate** - % unsubscribed (should be below 0.5%)

### Warning Signs

Take action if you see:

🚨 **Delivery rate drops below 95%**

- Check DNS records
- Review content for spam triggers
- Verify domain reputation

🚨 **Spam complaints above 0.1%**

- Review your signup process
- Ensure clear expectations
- Improve content quality

🚨 **Unsubscribe rate above 1%**

- Content may not match expectations
- Sending too frequently
- Poor targeting

### Test Before Sending

1. **Send test emails** - Check formatting and links
2. **Test multiple clients** - Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail
3. **Check spam score** - Use tools like Mail-Tester
4. **Preview on mobile** - Most emails are opened on mobile

## Troubleshooting Spam Issues

### If Your Emails Are Going to Spam

**Immediate actions:**

1. **Check DNS records** - Verify SPF, DKIM, DMARC are correct
2. **Review recent content** - Look for spam triggers
3. **Check sender reputation** - Use tools like SenderScore
4. **Reduce sending volume** - Temporarily slow down
5. **Contact support** - Penna can help investigate

**Long-term fixes:**

1. **Clean your list** - Remove inactive and invalid addresses
2. **Improve engagement** - Send better content
3. **Segment better** - More targeted sends
4. **Consistent schedule** - Regular, predictable sends
5. **Build reputation** - Takes time, stay consistent

### Recovery Plan

If your domain is flagged:

1. **Stop sending immediately**
2. **Identify the issue** - Content, list quality, complaints?
3. **Fix the root cause**
4. **Clean your list thoroughly**
5. **Request delisting** - If on blocklists
6. **Restart slowly** - Warm up again like new domain
7. **Monitor closely** - Watch metrics daily

## Advanced Tips

### Segment for Better Engagement

Send targeted content to engaged segments:

- Recent signups
- Active readers
- Specific interests
- Geographic regions

### Re-engagement Campaigns

For inactive subscribers:

1. Send "Are you still interested?" email
2. Offer to update preferences
3. Remove if no response

### Use Plain Text Versions

Some users prefer plain text. Always include:

- Simple formatting
- Clear links
- Minimal styling

### Authenticate Your "From" Address

Use an email address at your domain:

- ✅ newsletter@yourdomain.com

### Include Physical Address

Many countries require a physical address in commercial emails. Add yours in the footer.

✅ Custom domain configured  
✅ Subject line under 50 chars, no spam words  
✅ Content provides clear value  
✅ Text-to-image ratio is good (80/20)  
✅ Unsubscribe link included  
✅ Tested in multiple email clients  
✅ Links all work  
✅ Sent to engaged segment
