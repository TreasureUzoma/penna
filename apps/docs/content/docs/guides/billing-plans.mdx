# Billing & Plans

This guide covers Penna's pricing structure, how to upgrade or downgrade, and managing your billing.

## Managing Your Subscription

### Viewing Current Plan

1. Go to **Settings** → **Billing**
2. See your current plan details
3. View usage statistics
4. Check next billing date

### Upgrading Your Plan

**When to upgrade:**

- Approaching subscriber limit
- Need more sends per day
- Want advanced features
- Need better support

**How to upgrade:**

1. Go to **Settings** → **Billing**
2. Click **"Upgrade Plan"**
3. Select new plan
4. Review pricing
5. Confirm upgrade
6. Updated immediately

**Billing:**

- Prorated charge for remainder of period
- New rate starts next billing cycle
- No service interruption

### Downgrading Your Plan

**When to downgrade:**

- Lower subscriber count
- Reduced sending needs
- Budget constraints
- Feature needs changed

**How to downgrade:**

1. Go to **Settings** → **Billing**
2. Click **"Change Plan"**
3. Select lower plan
4. Review limitations
5. Confirm downgrade
6. Takes effect next billing cycle

**Important:**

- ⚠️ Downgrade effective at end of current period
- ⚠️ Check new limits before downgrading
- ⚠️ May lose access to certain features
- ⚠️ Excess subscribers may be suppressed

### Canceling Subscription

To cancel (return to Hobby plan):

1. Go to **Settings** → **Billing**
2. Click **"Cancel Subscription"**
3. Select reason (optional)
4. Confirm cancellation
5. Access until end of billing period

**After cancellation:**

- Reverts to Hobby plan limits
- 1,000 subscriber limit
- 3 sends per day limit
- Data retained
- Can resubscribe anytime

## Billing & Payments

### Payment Methods

**Accepted:**

- Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex)
- Debit cards
- PayPal (coming soon)
- Wire transfer (Enterprise only)

### Billing Cycles

**Monthly:**

- Billed on the same date each month
- Cancel anytime
- No long-term commitment

**Annual:** (Coming Soon)

- Save 20% vs monthly
- Billed once per year
- Better for established newsletters

### Managing Payment Methods

**Update payment method:**

1. Go to **Settings** → **Billing**
2. Click **"Payment Method"**
3. Add new card or update existing
4. Set as default
5. Save changes

**Multiple payment methods:**

- Add backup payment method
- Switch between methods
- Remove old methods

### Invoices & Receipts

**Access invoices:**

1. Go to **Settings** → **Billing** → **Invoices**
2. View all past invoices
3. Download PDF
4. Email invoice to accounting

**Invoice details:**

- Date and amount
- Plan details
- Tax information (if applicable)
- Company information

### Failed Payments

**If payment fails:**

1. Email notification sent
2. Retry attempted in 3 days
3. Second retry after 6 days
4. Account downgraded if still failing

**Resolve quickly:**

- Update payment method
- Check card expiration
- Ensure sufficient funds
- Contact support if issues

## Usage & Limits

### Monitoring Usage

**Track in dashboard:**

- Current subscriber count vs limit
- Newsletter sends used today
- API requests this month
- Storage used (if applicable)

**Usage notifications:**

- 80% of limit - Warning email
- 95% of limit - Urgent warning
- 100% of limit - Upgrade required

### Handling Limit Overages

**Subscribers over limit:**

- New signups queued
- Upgrade to activate
- Or remove inactive subscribers

**Sends over daily limit:**

- Blocked until next day
- Upgrade for higher limit
- Or split sends across days

**Soft vs Hard Limits:**

- Soft: Warning, still functional
- Hard: Blocked until resolved

## Billing FAQs

**Q: Can I change plans anytime?**
A: Yes, upgrade anytime (immediate) or downgrade (next cycle).

**Q: What happens to my data if I downgrade?**
A: Data is retained, but you may hit new limits.

**Q: Do you offer refunds?**
A: Refunds on case-by-case basis within 30 days.

**Q: Can I pause my subscription?**
A: No pause feature, but you can downgrade to Hobby.

**Q: Is there a contract?**
A: No long-term contract for monthly plans.

**Q: Do prices include tax?**
A: Tax may be added based on location.

**Q: Can I pay annually?**
A: Annual billing coming soon with discount.

**Q: What if I exceed limits?**
A: Automatic upgrade prompt or service limitations.
