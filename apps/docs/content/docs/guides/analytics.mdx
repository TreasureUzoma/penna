## Current Tracking

While comprehensive analytics are in development, you can currently track:

### Basic Metrics

**In your dashboard:**

- Total subscribers
- Newsletter sends
- Growth trends

### Manual Tracking

**Track externally using:**

**UTM parameters in links:**

```
https://yoursite.com/article?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=weekly_01
```

**Use Google Analytics or your analytics platform to track:**

- Click-through rates
- Conversion rates
- Traffic from newsletters
- User behavior after clicking

**Track engagement signals:**

- Replies to your newsletters
- Social media mentions
- Support inquiries referencing newsletters

## Best Practices for When Analytics Launch

**Prepare now:**

1. **Consistent naming** - Use clear, consistent newsletter titles
2. **UTM tracking** - Add UTM parameters to all links
3. **Baseline metrics** - Track basic metrics manually to compare
4. **Goals definition** - Define what success looks like
5. **Regular schedule** - Consistent sending for better trend analysis

## What to Measure

### Primary Metrics

**Open Rate**

- Industry average: 15-30%
- Higher is better, but context matters
- Track trends over time

**Click-Through Rate**

- Industry average: 2-5%
- Measures content relevance
- Track which content drives clicks

**Unsubscribe Rate**

- Target: below 0.5%
- Higher rate signals problems
- Normal after major changes

### Secondary Metrics

**Engagement Over Time**

- Are new subscribers engaging?
- When does engagement drop?
- Which cohorts perform best?

**Content Performance**

- What topics drive engagement?
- What format works best?
- Optimal content length?

**Send Time Optimization**

- Best day of week?
- Best time of day?
- Timezone considerations?
